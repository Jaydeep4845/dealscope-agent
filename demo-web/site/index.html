How well does it work? Benchmark, method and limitations

355 questions from 6 companies never seen in training (Chevron, Johnson & Johnson, Meta, Microsoft, Oracle, Walmart). Each question is ranked against all 355 passages from those companies; exactly one passage is correct.

MetricBase bge-smallFine-tunedChange

How it was built: 10-K filings from SEC EDGAR, split into passages from Items 1, 1A, 7 and 7A; an LLM wrote one analyst-style question per passage; the split is by company to avoid leakage; fine-tuned with MultipleNegativesRankingLoss (in-batch negatives) on a T4 GPU. In this page the models run as int8 ONNX through Transformers.js, passage vectors are precomputed, and only your question is encoded live. Limitations: the training questions are synthetic; only narrative 10-K text is covered; free-form questions have no ground truth, so judge the passages by reading them. Similarity scores are on different scales for the two models, so compare passages, not numbers.
