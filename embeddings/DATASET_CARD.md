# SEC 10-K Retrieval QA

Question-passage pairs built from the latest annual reports (Form 10-K) of 24 large US public companies,
for training and evaluating retrieval (embedding) models on financial filings.

Built for [dealscope-agent](https://github.com/Jaydeep4845/dealscope-agent), where it is used to fine-tune
[jc4845/bge-small-sec-filings](https://huggingface.co/jc4845/bge-small-sec-filings).

| Split | Pairs | Companies |
|---|---|---|
| train | 1,067 | 18 |
| test | 355 | 6 (CVX, JNJ, META, MSFT, ORCL, WMT) |

**The split is by company**, so test questions come only from companies never seen in training. This avoids
leakage of company-specific vocabulary and measures how a model generalises to new filings.

## Fields

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `id` | `TICKER-n` |
| `ticker` | Company ticker |
| `section` | 10-K section: Item 1 (Business), 1A (Risk Factors), 7 (MD&A), 7A (Market Risk) |
| `filing_date` | Filing date of the 10-K |
| `source_url` | Link to the filing on SEC EDGAR |
| `question` | Analyst-style question that the passage answers |
| `passage` | 120-220 word passage from the filing |

Example:

> **question:** What revenue does MSFT expect to recognize from contracts whose performance obligations have not
> yet been transferred to customers?
> **passage (Item 7):** Remaining performance obligations represent the revenue we expect to recognize for our
> products and services for which control has not yet been transferred to customers. ...

## How it was built

1. Latest 10-K per company downloaded from the SEC EDGAR API (with a contact User-Agent, under the SEC rate limit).
2. HTML converted to text (tables removed); Items 1, 1A, 7 and 7A split into 120-220 word passages; up to 60
   passages sampled per company.
3. One question per passage generated by open-weight LLMs (gpt-oss-20b, gpt-oss-120b, Qwen) via Groq, in batches
   of 5 with JSON output validation. The prompt asks for a specific analyst question that does not copy long
   phrases from the passage.
4. Split by company into train and test.

Code: [embeddings/build_dataset.py](https://github.com/Jaydeep4845/dealscope-agent/tree/main/embeddings).

## Limitations

- Questions are **synthetic** (LLM-generated). They are cleaner and more specific than real user queries, and some
  combine two questions in one.
- Only narrative text from four 10-K sections; no tables, 10-Qs or earnings calls.
- Some companies are missing because their filings use heading formats the parser does not recognise
  (for example Amazon and Intel).
- Each question has exactly one labelled passage; other passages may also partially answer it.
